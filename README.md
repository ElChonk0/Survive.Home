# Survive.Home

A household emergency-readiness checklist that scales to the people (and pets) in your home. It works offline once opened, and can be installed to a phone's home screen.

- **Start here**: what to get first when starting from nothing, in stages.
- **Full checklist**: 193 items ordered by what keeps people alive first, with how and when to use each one, and where popular prepping channels disagree, their alternative.

Everything is stored in your browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
