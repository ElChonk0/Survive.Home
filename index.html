Quantities follow the playlist's figures where it gives them: 1 gal of water per person per day, 14 days of water, and the 90-day staples ratios from video 24. Calorie targets use standard dietary-guideline estimates by age, or, when you enter height and weight, the Institute of Medicine energy equations for children and Mifflin–St Jeor × 1.5 for adults (a light-to-moderate work day). Drinking water scales with weight at ½ oz per lb, never below 1 gal per person (pregnancy +340 kcal in the 2nd trimester and +450 in the 3rd). Items marked “outside playlist” fill gaps the videos don't cover, mostly pregnancy and small children, using FEMA, CDC, AAP, ACOG and Red Cross public guidance.
